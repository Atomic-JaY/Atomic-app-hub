WHERE PHOTOS GO
---------------
This "photos" folder must sit in the same folder as "Life Safety PM Tracker.html".

    Life Safety PM Tracker.html
    Life Safety PM Log.xlsx
    photos/            <-- this folder
        FE1.jpg
        FE1-loc.jpg
        ...

Drop a photo in, then open (or reopen) the tracker and click that marker. Nothing
needs rebuilding. If a photo is missing, the card tells you the filename it wants.

There is also a single-file version, "Life Safety PM Tracker (single file).html",
sent alongside this zip rather than inside it. All 96 photos are built into that
one file. Use it for email, SharePoint, or a shared drive, where a sibling folder
would not travel with the file. Use the folder version here when you want to keep
adding or swapping photos yourself.


HOW TO NAME EACH FILE
---------------------
Use the device ID exactly as it appears on the marker.

    FE1.jpg          close-up of the unit
    FE1-loc.jpg      wide shot showing where it sits on the wall
    FE1-2.jpg        any extra shot (optional)

There is no tag slot. The sign-off record lives in the tracker itself, so
re-photographing tags every month would only duplicate it.

Same pattern for the other two types:

    EL7.jpg   EL7-loc.jpg     emergency light EL7
    EW2.jpg   EW2-loc.jpg     eye wash station EW2

Case matters on the ID: use FE1, not fe1.

Full ID list: FE1 - FE21, EL1 - EL23, EW1 - EW4.

COMPLETE: all 48 devices photographed - FE1-FE21, EL1-EL23, EW1-EW4.
96 photos, a close-up and a location shot for each.

To replace or improve any of them, just drop a new file over the old one
using the same name.


FILE TYPES THAT WORK
--------------------
    .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp

HEIC DOES NOT WORK. iPhones shoot HEIC by default and browsers cannot display it.
Fix it once, before you start:

    Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible


SIZE
----
Straight-off-the-phone photos are far larger than needed. The ones already in here
were reduced to 1200x1600, about 300 KB each, which is plenty for the card and the
full-screen view. Send them at full size and they will be reduced on the way in.


WHAT THE PHOTOS ESTABLISHED
---------------------------
Everything the log was missing has now been read off these photos and is in the
workbook:

    - Agent TYPE for all 21 extinguishers. Sixteen are dry chemical and five are
      CO2: FE2, FE4, FE5, FE6, FE7. That distinction matters - CO2 cylinders get a
      5-year hydrostatic test, dry chemical 12-year, so it changes those due dates
      by seven years.

    - DATE OF MANUFACTURE. The shell stamps read ZX26 and the collar tags are
      marked 2026, so the cylinders are 2026 units. NFPA 10 runs the internal-exam
      and hydrostatic-test clocks from date of manufacture, and the log now does
      too, using 01/01/2026 - the earliest date the stamp allows, so the due dates
      it produces are the most conservative ones. If you ever find a month on a
      shell, put the real date in the "Mfd Date" column and both clocks move with
      it.

    - LAST ANNUAL MAINTENANCE for the 11 UniFirst-tagged units (FE9, FE10, FE12
      through FE20), confirmed as March 2026.

The remaining 10 "Untracked" entries on the FE tab are the annual maintenance on
the 10 units that carry no UniFirst tag. Those are the ones to chase.

WHAT IS STILL WORTH A LOOK
--------------------------
    - EW2 is a squeeze-bottle station, and it is signed as an emergency eyewash at
      the hi-lo battery chargers. Under ANSI/ISEA Z358.1 bottles are supplemental
      only; they do not satisfy the 15-minute flush a battery-acid splash needs.
      A plumbed or self-contained unit is what that location calls for.

    - Z358.1 asks for a weekly activation of plumbed eyewash stations, not monthly.
      The log is set to weekly, which is why all four read overdue against the
      07/23/2026 baseline. That is the standard talking, not a bug - the fix is a
      weekly walk, not a longer interval.
